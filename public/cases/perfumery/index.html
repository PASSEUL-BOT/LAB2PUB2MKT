Understanding AccordsComprendre les accords理解香氣和弦

What Goes Well Together?Quelles odeurs vont bien ensemble ?香氣搭配實驗室

The idea L’idée 原理

The lab tests pairings. You choose ingredients, a five-question guide answers with an evidence label (documented, inference, hypothesis or not enough information), and charts show the profile. There is no score, on purpose.

實驗室測試搭配：回答附上依據等級，不打分數。

How to use this page Comment l’utiliser 操作方式

  1. Step 1: pick families on the wheel. 步驟 1：在輪盤選家族。
  2. Step 2: add ingredients from the list. Reorder or remove them in your combination. 步驟 2：加入材料，可調整順序或移除。
  3. Steps 3 to 5: read the guide, the radar and the map, then the summary. 步驟 3–5：看指南、雷達圖、關係圖與摘要。

One system, five steps: the wheel, the picker, the guide and the charts all read the same data, so they never contradict each other. Un seul système en cinq étapes : la roue, le sélecteur, le guide et les graphiques partagent les mêmes données et ne se contredisent jamais.

Safety. This lab is on screen only. If you ever smell real materials: ask an adult, keep the room ventilated, never taste them, never put concentrated materials on skin, and stop if anything irritates you. “Natural” does not mean harmless and “synthetic” does not mean harmful. Si vous sentez un jour de vraies matières : demandez à un adulte, aérez, ne goûtez jamais, n’appliquez jamais de matière concentrée sur la peau.

How the lab reasons: the public rules Les règles du laboratoire 運算規則
  1. Pairs. Every pair of chosen ingredients is checked. Two ingredients of the same family are an “Educational Inference”. Two ingredients of different families use the curated family relation, the same one the wheel shows. If none is curated the pair is “Insufficient Data”; a cautious hypothesis may be shown, labelled as such.
  2. Profile. Each of the seven dimensions is a weighted mean of the author-set teaching values (0–5). The first ingredient weighs the most: weights are 1 + 0.25 × (number of ingredients − position). Intensity adds 0.25 per extra ingredient, up to 5.
  3. Direction. “What might the result smell like” compares the blend with the same blend without its last ingredient. A direction appears when the change is at least 0.3 (0.4 for Warmer, 0.5 for Drier).
  4. Tension warnings appear only when their written condition is true, for example strong sweetness next to a light fresh top note.
  5. No compatibility score. A single number would look like a measurement. The lab shows evidence labels instead: Documented Pairing, Educational Inference, Experimental Hypothesis, Insufficient Data.
  6. Limits. These are teaching estimates, not measurements. Real blends depend on amounts, quality and your own nose, so only smelling can confirm anything.

From Blend to BottleDe l’assemblage au flacon從混合液到瓶子

The idea L’idée 原理

A finished blend still has to be cleaned up, bottled and kept safe. This level follows it from the bench to a sealed bottle, and then to a factory.

配好的香水還要過濾、裝瓶、保存；這一級從實驗桌一路看到工廠。

How to use this page Comment l’utiliser 操作方式

  1. Read the six steps in order. 依序讀六個步驟。
  2. Open the question about antioxidants. 打開抗氧化劑的問題。
  3. Read the safety note. This is an explanation, not a recipe. 讀安全提醒；這裡是說明，不是配方。

This level follows alcohol-based perfume from the bench to the shop. The steps are general practice, simplified for learners. Real makers follow their own tested procedures and the rules of their country.
這些是簡化後的一般做法；真正的製造商有自己驗證過的程序，並遵守各國法規。

How a factory makes it: six steps Comment l’usine la fabrique : six étapes 工廠如何製作：六個步驟

  1. 1

    Buy pure ingredients and check them Acheter des matières pures et les contrôler 採購與檢驗

    We use very pure alcohol and perfume materials that meet their specifications. Before using them, we check for dirt, old smells and oxidation, which means the material has gone stale from air.

    使用高純度乙醇與符合規格的香料原料，並檢查雜質、氧化程度與品質。

  2. 2

    Mix by the exact recipe Mélanger selon la formule exacte 精準調配

    We mix the perfume materials and the alcohol in exact proportions. Sometimes we add an antioxidant, a helper that slows down the staling caused by air.

    依比例精準混合香料與乙醇，必要時加入抗氧化劑，降低氧化劣化的風險。

  3. 3

    Let it rest Laisser macérer 靜置熟成

    The mixture sits still under controlled conditions. This is called maceration. The smells blend together, and we watch whether the recipe stays steady.

    在受控條件下靜置（Maceration），讓香氣融合，並觀察配方是否穩定。

  4. 4

    Chill and filter Refroidir et filtrer 低溫處理與過濾

    If the recipe needs it, we make it cold. Bits that do not dissolve sink or clump together, and we filter them out so the liquid becomes clear.

    視配方需要進行低溫處理，去除沉澱與不溶物，讓液體更澄清。

  5. 5

    Test quality and stability Tester la qualité et la stabilité 品質與穩定性測試

    We check the smell, colour and clarity. Then we test how it ages: a fast test with warmth and light, and a long test over many months.

    檢查香氣、顏色與澄清度，並做加速老化與長期穩定性評估。

  6. 6

    Fill, seal and pack Remplir, fermer, emballer 密封充填與包裝

    We use bottles and spray heads that do not react with the perfume, a tight seal, and packaging that blocks light. This protects the perfume from light, air and evaporation.

    使用相容性良好的瓶器、密封噴頭與遮光包裝，降低光線、空氣與揮發造成的劣化。

Can a trace of antioxidant be added for long keeping? Peut-on ajouter une trace d’antioxydant ? 能加微量抗氧化劑長期保存嗎？

Sometimes, yes. Air slowly changes perfume materials, so a scent can turn stale or yellow. An antioxidant is a helper that slows this down, and makers may add a very small amount. A perfume with a lot of alcohol already protects itself from germs, so it usually needs no preservative. Choosing the antioxidant and deciding how much is a trained formulator’s job, and safety rules limit the amount. This site gives no amounts.

有時可以。空氣會慢慢改變香料，讓香味變質或顏色變黃；抗氧化劑能減緩這個過程，廠商有時會加極微量。酒精比例高的香水本身就能抑制細菌，通常不需要防腐劑。選哪種抗氧化劑、加多少，由受過訓練的配方師決定，並受安全法規限制。本站不提供用量。

Safety. Real perfume contains alcohol and strong materials. Only an adult, following proper safety rules, should make or bottle it. 真正的香水含酒精與強烈原料，只有大人在符合安全規範下才能製作與裝瓶。
