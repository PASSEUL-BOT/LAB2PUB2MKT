HOW TO OPEN / 開啟方式
1. Right-click the ZIP -> Extract All (解壓縮全部). Do NOT open files from inside the ZIP.
2. In the extracted folder, double-click  perfumery-standalone.html  (Chrome or Edge).
   Keep the "photos" folder next to it.
3. Do not use index.html by double-click, and do not preview in Google Drive / email preview.
If the page shows a red bar saying the interactive parts did not start, follow it.
Developer version: run  python -m http.server  in this folder, then open http://localhost:8000
